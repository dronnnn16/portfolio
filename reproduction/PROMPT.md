# Build prompt: Chapter 6 Reproduction revision page

This is the prompt the page in this folder was built from.

---

**Role.** You are a frontend designer and a Class 10 science teacher. Build one self-contained web page that a student can use to revise "Chapter 6: Reproduction (How do organisms reproduce?)" for the RBSE board exam and school tests. It follows the NCERT syllabus that RBSE uses.

**Audience.** Class 10 students, Hindi and English medium. Phone first, but also comfortable on a laptop.

**Content to cover (accurate to NCERT).**
1. Why organisms reproduce: DNA copying, variation and why it helps a species survive.
2. Asexual reproduction: binary fission (Amoeba, Leishmania), multiple fission (Plasmodium), fragmentation (Spirogyra), regeneration (Planaria, Hydra), budding (Hydra, yeast), spore formation (Rhizopus), vegetative propagation (Bryophyllum, potato, sugarcane, rose, banana; layering, grafting) and its advantages.
3. Sexual reproduction in flowering plants: flower parts, unisexual and bisexual flowers, self and cross pollination, pollen tube, fertilisation, what becomes seed and fruit, germination.
4. Human reproduction: puberty changes, male system (testes, scrotum, testosterone, vas deferens, seminal vesicles, prostate, urethra, penis), female system (ovaries, oviduct, uterus, cervix, vagina), fertilisation, implantation, placenta, menstruation.
5. Reproductive health: contraception (barrier, hormonal, IUCD, surgical), sexually transmitted diseases (bacterial and viral), the ban on prenatal sex determination and the sex ratio.
6. Give the Hindi term next to each key English term.

**Interactive features (all must work).**
- A 3D viewer built with Three.js. Three models: male reproductive system, female reproductive system, and a condom. Realistic shading (physically based materials, environment lighting, soft shadows, procedural tissue textures). Drag to rotate a full 360°, pinch or scroll to zoom, auto-rotate toggle, reset view, labels toggle, a section (cut-away) view, fullscreen. Clickable labels that show each part's name, Hindi name, function and an exam tip. An animated trace of the path of sperm (male) and egg (female). An unroll slider for the condom.
- Animated stage player for asexual reproduction: binary fission, multiple fission, budding, regeneration, spore formation, with play, pause, a stage slider and captions.
- Clickable longitudinal section of a flower, with an animated pollen tube.
- Comparison tables in tabs.
- Flip flashcards.
- A 20-question MCQ quiz with explanations, score, review of mistakes and a saved best score.
- Board-style questions grouped by marks, each with a hidden model answer.
- A revision checklist (topics and must-draw diagrams) that saves progress in the browser.
- Search that filters the notes, and a "key points only" mode for last-minute revision.

**Design.** A distinctive look taken from the subject itself: the colours of a stained microscope slide (haematoxylin indigo and eosin pink on pale glass; a dark-field look in dark mode). A characterful display face, a highly legible body face, and a Devanagari face for Hindi terms. Works in light and dark themes, at phone width with no sideways scroll, with visible keyboard focus and reduced motion respected.

**Constraints.** One HTML page. No external images. Scripts only from approved CDNs, pinned to exact versions. The content must be suitable for school students: textbook-style anatomy, no explicit imagery.
