A gym built by a coach, not a franchise.

Aaron Chaudhry grew up in Jaipur, training in the akhadas of the old city before spending a decade coaching strength athletes in Delhi and Bengaluru. In 2016 he came home and opened Iron Haveli with one idea: a serious gym where every member is known by name and nobody trains without a plan.

Today the floor has competition-grade racks, calibrated plates, a boxing corner and a turf lane for sled work. What hasn’t changed is the coaching. Every new member starts with a movement assessment with Aaron or one of his certified coaches, and gets a programme written for their body and their goals.

“Most people don’t need a fancier gym. They need someone to show them how to train, and to notice when they don’t turn up.”

— Aaron Chaudhry

What you can train here

Every membership includes open gym access and all group classes. Personal training is an add-on.

  • Strength & powerlifting

    Squat, bench and deadlift with coached technique and a progressive plan. Twelve racks, so you never queue.

    Coached blocks on Mon, Wed and Fri

  • Functional conditioning

    45-minute group sessions with kettlebells, sleds, rowers and bodyweight work. Built for fitness you can feel on the stairs.

    Daily, 6 am, 7 am and 7 pm

  • Boxing

    Footwork, bag rounds and partner drills. Beginners are welcome and gloves are provided for your first month.

    Tue, Thu and Sat at 6 pm

  • Women-only morning batch

    A dedicated floor and a woman coach from 6 to 9 am, for anyone who prefers to train in a women-only space.

    Mon to Sat, 6–9 am

  • Personal training

    One-to-one sessions with Aaron or a senior coach, for fat loss, injury rehab, sport-specific prep or your first competition.

    By appointment
